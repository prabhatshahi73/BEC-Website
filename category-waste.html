Circular economyDraft

Waste, Plastics and Chemicals · Global

A circular economy keeps products, components and materials in use for as long as possible and designs waste out of the system. It is usually contrasted with the linear model of take, make and dispose.

Extended Producer Responsibility (India)Draft

Waste, Plastics and Chemicals · India

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) makes the producers, importers and brand owners of a product responsible for managing it after it is used. In India, EPR rules cover plastic packaging, electronic waste and batteries, among others.

Hazardous waste management (India)Draft

Waste, Plastics and Chemicals · India

In India, hazardous waste is regulated under the Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, 2016. Anyone who generates, stores, transports, treats or disposes of such waste needs authorisation and must follow the handling rules.

Plastic pollutionDraft

Waste, Plastics and Chemicals · Global

Plastic pollution is the build-up of plastic waste in land, rivers and oceans. Plastic breaks down very slowly, and it fragments into microplastics that spread through water, soil, air and food chains.
