    Frequently asked questions

     

    How do I pay?

    Fixed-price services are paid online at checkout. For services that need a scope review, we send a quote on the portal and you pay after approving it.

    How is my work delivered?

    Your final report is uploaded to your order on the portal. Status updates and all messages with your consultant also stay there, so everything for an order is in one place.

    How long does a quote take?

    Submit your requirements from the service page. Our team reviews the scope and posts a quote and expected timeline on your order.

    What if I need changes to a report?

    Message your consultant from the order page. Revisions are handled on the same order and the updated report is uploaded there.

    Can I order for more than one site or unit?

    Yes. Mention each site in your order notes or request a quote, and we will scope them together.
