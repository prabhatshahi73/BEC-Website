# Apply before re-applying to AdSense
1. Replace `editor@bharatenvironmentconsultants.com` in contact.html and privacy-policy.html with your real editorial inbox.
2. Upload all files to the repo root (they overwrite about/contact/privacy-policy). Keep your existing terms.html but rebrand it "Bharat Environment News" and link it (it is already in the footer).
3. Add to your news.html and every article footer: links to Editorial Policy and a "Privacy settings" link (Google Privacy & messaging consent tool, AdSense > Privacy & messaging).
4. Every article: named byline (link to About), publication + updated dates, inline hyperlinks to sources, credit line for every image (author + licence). Replace Wikimedia hotlinks that lack credit. Fix empty Figure images and the "A empowered" typo in the Delhi article.
5. Cloudflare: turn off Bot Fight Mode/challenges for Googlebot and Mediapartners-Google. Submit sitemap.xml in Search Console and confirm articles are indexed.
6. Put news on its own subdomain or domain, or make the root page a genuine content hub, then apply AdSense on that.
7. After approval: put your real pub ID in ads.txt and upload to the site root.
