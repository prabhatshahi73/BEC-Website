Consent to Establish and Consent to Operate (India)Draft

Law, Policy and Governance · India

Consent to Establish (CTE) and Consent to Operate (CTO) are permissions that industries in India must obtain from the State Pollution Control Board, under the Water Act and the Air Act, before they are built and before they start operating.

Environmental Clearance (India)Draft

Law, Policy and Governance · India

Environmental Clearance (EC) is the approval that certain new or expanding projects in India must obtain, after an impact assessment, before they can start. It is required under the Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Notification of 2006.

Forest Conservation Act (India)Draft

Law, Policy and Governance · India

The Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 restricts the use of forest land for non-forest purposes in India without prior approval of the Central Government. An amendment in 2023 renamed it the Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam, 1980 and changed which lands it covers.

Polluter pays principleDraft

Law, Policy and Governance · Global

The polluter pays principle says that whoever causes pollution should bear the cost of preventing it, controlling it and repairing the harm it causes. It is recognised in international environmental law and is part of Indian law.
