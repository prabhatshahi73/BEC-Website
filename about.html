About Bharat Environment News

Bharat Environment News reports on environmental regulation, pollution data, climate policy, biodiversity and waste management in India. We turn scattered government datasets, audit reports and regulatory filings into articles that residents, compliance officers and policymakers can use.

Who publishes this site

The site is published by Bharat Environment Consultants (BEC), an environmental compliance consultancy founded in 2005 in Gurugram. Editorial decisions are made separately from BEC's consulting work. News articles do not advertise BEC services, and BEC clients do not influence coverage.

Editor

Prabhat Kumar Shahi, Founder of BEC and Editor of Bharat Environment News. He has worked in Indian environmental compliance since 2005, covering CPCB and MoEFCC processes, NGT matters, EIA Notification 2006, hazardous waste, CRZ, bio-medical waste and solid waste rules.
