Who We Are

Founded in 2005, Bharat Environment Consultants (BEC) is one of India's most trusted environmental consultancy firms, headquartered in Gurugram, Haryana. We have built a two-decade legacy of delivering science-backed environmental advisory to industries, infrastructure developers, and government bodies across 18 Indian states.

We bridge the gap between regulatory compliance and genuine ecological stewardship — empowering our clients to operate sustainably, meet regulatory obligations, and contribute meaningfully to India's environmental goals.

Our multidisciplinary team of ecologists, engineers, legal experts, GIS specialists, hydrologists, and sustainability strategists brings deep expertise to every engagement — from small industrial units to large-scale national infrastructure projects.

Our Mission

To guide India's industries and institutions towards environmental responsibility through science-led advisory, regulatory expertise, and a commitment to measurable ecological impact — helping build a greener, more compliant, and sustainable India.
