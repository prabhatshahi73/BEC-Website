Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR)Draft

Sustainability and Reporting · India

The Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR) is the format in which listed companies in India disclose their environmental, social and governance performance. It was introduced by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) in 2021.

Greenhouse gas emission scopes (Scope 1, 2 and 3)Draft

Sustainability and Reporting · Global

Scope 1, 2 and 3 are the three categories that the Greenhouse Gas Protocol uses to classify an organisation's greenhouse gas emissions, depending on whether they come directly from the organisation or from its energy use and value chain.
