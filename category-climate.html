Climate changeDraft

Climate and Atmosphere · Global

Climate change is the long-term shift in Earth's temperatures and weather patterns. Since the 1800s, human activity, mainly the burning of coal, oil and gas, has been the main driver.

Ozone layer and the Montreal ProtocolDraft

Climate and Atmosphere · Global

The ozone layer is a region of the stratosphere that absorbs most of the Sun's harmful ultraviolet-B radiation. The Montreal Protocol of 1987 phased out the chemicals that were destroying it and is widely regarded as one of the most successful environmental treaties.

Paris AgreementDraft

Climate and Atmosphere · Global

The Paris Agreement is an international treaty on climate change adopted in 2015. Its goal is to hold the rise in global average temperature to well below 2 °C above pre-industrial levels and to pursue efforts to limit it to 1.5 °C.
