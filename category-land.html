Biodiversity lossDraft

Land, Forests and Biodiversity · Global

Biodiversity loss is the decline in the variety of life on Earth, including the number of species, the genetic variety within them and the health of ecosystems. Scientific assessments identify human activity as its main cause.

Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity FrameworkDraft

Land, Forests and Biodiversity · Global

The Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework is the global plan to halt and reverse biodiversity loss, adopted by the countries of the Convention on Biological Diversity in December 2022. It sets four goals for 2050 and 23 targets for 2030.
