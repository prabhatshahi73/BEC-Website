Environmental Compensation (EC) Calculator

Estimate the Environmental Compensation payable for violations of the Water (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act and Air (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act, based on the CPCB In-house Committee's Methodology for Assessing Environmental Compensation.

Applicable to Red, Orange and Green category industrial units directed to pay EC by CPCB/SPCB for non-compliance, pending closure, or violation of consent conditions.

1 Industry Category (Pollution Index)

CPCB Pollution Index (2016 categorisation) used as the base severity factor. White category units are generally exempt from EC under this methodology.

2 Unit & Violation Details

Based on investment/turnover classification (MSME Act) of the unit.
Total number of days the unit operated in violation / non-compliance.
Location Factor as per the unit's proximity to habitation / sensitive area (latest Census).
Fixed base rate as prescribed by the CPCB Committee report.
