Transitioning to Renewable Energy: A Guide for Indian Corporates

By BEC Experts  |  6 min read  |  May 2025

Solar and wind energy are now cheaper than coal across most of India. For Indian corporates, switching to renewable energy is no longer just about sustainability — it is a financially smart decision that reduces electricity bills, locks in predictable energy costs, and satisfies growing stakeholder expectations.

☀️ India's solar energy tariffs have fallen by over 90% in the last decade. At ₹2–₹3 per unit, solar is now India's cheapest source of electricity.

Three Main Pathways to Renewable Energy

🏭

Rooftop Solar

Install solar panels on factory or office rooftops. Best for medium to large facilities with high daytime electricity consumption. Payback period: 4–6 years.

📄

Power Purchase Agreement (PPA)

Sign a long-term contract to buy renewable electricity directly from a generator at a fixed price. No upfront capital required. Ideal for large power consumers.

🏅

Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs)

Purchase RECs to claim renewable energy usage without physical supply change. Good for meeting ESG reporting requirements and green claims quickly.

Financial Benefits Beyond Energy Savings

Transitioning to renewable energy unlocks access to green financing at preferential rates from banks and development finance institutions. The Reserve Bank of India has expanded its priority sector lending to include renewable energy projects, and international green bonds are increasingly available to Indian corporates with credible sustainability credentials.

Companies that can demonstrate renewable energy usage also gain a competitive advantage in global supply chains, where multinational buyers increasingly require suppliers to report and reduce Scope 3 emissions.
