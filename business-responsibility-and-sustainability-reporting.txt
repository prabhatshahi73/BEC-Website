## Purpose
BRSR asks companies to report on how they manage environmental and social impacts alongside financial results, so that investors and other stakeholders can compare them. It replaced the earlier Business Responsibility Report.

## Who must report
SEBI made BRSR mandatory for the top 1,000 listed companies by market capitalisation from the financial year 2022-23. Other companies may report voluntarily. SEBI has revised the scope and added requirements since then, so check the current regulations.

## Structure
The report has three sections.
- **Section A** gives general disclosures about the company.
- **Section B** describes management processes, policies and governance.
- **Section C** reports performance against the nine principles of the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct, using essential indicators that all reporting companies must answer and leadership indicators that are optional.

## Environmental disclosures
Under the principle on the environment, companies report on energy use, water use and discharge, air emissions, waste and Scope 1 and Scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions (see [[greenhouse-gas-scopes]]).

## Assurance
SEBI later introduced a BRSR Core set of key indicators that require independent assurance. The list of indicators and the phasing of assurance have been updated over time. Check the latest SEBI circulars before preparing a report.
