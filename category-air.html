Fine particulate matter (PM2.5)Draft

Air Pollution and Quality · Global

PM2.5 is the name for airborne particles smaller than 2.5 micrometres across. They are small enough to travel deep into the lungs and bloodstream, and they are among the air pollutants most strongly linked to ill health.

National Air Quality Index (India)Draft

Air Pollution and Quality · India

The National Air Quality Index (NAQI) is India's system for turning air pollutant measurements into one number and a colour-coded category, from Good to Severe. It was launched in 2014 and is run by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB).
