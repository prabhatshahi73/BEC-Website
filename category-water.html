Ocean acidificationDraft

Water and Oceans · Global

Ocean acidification is the ongoing fall in the pH of the ocean, caused mainly by the ocean absorbing carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. It makes it harder for shell-building and reef-building organisms to form their skeletons.
