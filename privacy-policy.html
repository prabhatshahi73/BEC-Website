Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense. Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before setting advertising cookies. You can change your choice at any time through the privacy settings link in the footer. You can also block cookies in your browser, which may affect how the site works.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it with hosting and analytics providers, with Google for advertising, and where the law requires.

Retention and security

Messages are kept for up to three years. We use reasonable technical measures to protect data, but no system is completely secure.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent or complain about our handling of it, subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Write to our grievance contact: editor@bharatenvironmentconsultants.com.
